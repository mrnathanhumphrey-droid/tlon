## ⭐⭐ WHERE THE PUZZLE TRACK ACTUALLY IS

### What was wrong, and it was never the weights

Two failures, both in the corpus, neither visible in any output — the loss was
healthy and the tests were green throughout.

1. **The speaker had never seen natural English.** `tlon/act2/corpus.py:282`
   built every write pair as `english = gloss(scene)`, the austere machine
   gloss. The puzzle sends *"I sat on a bench by the river"*, a register with
   zero training support.
2. **It was trained that context carries nothing.** `tlon/discourse/multiturn.py`
   says so in its own docstring: *"read ONLY the prior turn's force ... No
   accumulation, no content relation."* Correct for the research — it is the
   control that stops persistence being smuggled in by the sampler — and fatal
   for a puzzle.

⛔⛤ **THE FIRST REPAIR WAS BOUGHT ON A PROXY AND DID NOT WORK.** A new corpus
measured **68% content carry** and a retrain was paid for on that number. The
68% was WORD overlap, and three quarters of it was degree, tense and relator
particles — forms every utterance needs, so they collide by necessity. At ROOT
level, which is what a player decodes against, it was **9%**; *within* a
conversation it ran **below chance** (5.3% against a 7.5% shuffled null,
p=0.000 over 500 shuffles). ⭐ **MEASURE THE CORPUS WITH THE INSTRUMENT THE
PRODUCT IS JUDGED BY, BEFORE BUYING IT.**

### What fixed it: steer stage 2

Render the P line first, pass its roots into the reply's proposal as
`require_roots`, so the reply is **told** which root to carry.

```
arm        pairs   root-carry              echo
unsteered     44   40.9% [27.7, 55.6]       —
STEERED      203   97.0% [93.7, 98.6]      1.5%     CIs DO NOT OVERLAP
```

⭐⭐ **THE ORIGINAL FINDING IS INVERTED.** Roots were 9.3% of what carried and
degree led at 35.2%; steered, **ROOT LEADS AT 56.0%**.
