## ⭐⭐ What the machinery learned (all live, all tested)

1. **The stop halts on a READABLE state, not only GO.** `readable_stop()` +
   exit 4; the pipeline gates on `[ $E1 -eq 0 ] || [ $E1 -eq 4 ]`. Rung 1a's
   GO-only stop let it train past its own clean floor. Pinned as presence *and*
   absence — a revert would fail nothing else.
2. **Dose is measured PER PARAMETER.** `rms = delta_norm / sqrt(n_trainable)`.
   Raw `delta_norm` grows with parameter count, so matching it across a
   layer-count change under-doses the larger run. `9ccf98d6`'s band was in the
   wrong unit; its verdict survives, its unit does not.
3. **`fraction_changed` saturates** — identical to 16 digits across both of rung
   1a's epochs. Precondition only, never a dose.
4. **The verdict names its own prereg and re-hashes it.** Was hardcoded, so rung
   1b's artifact claimed the wrong pre-registration. `--prereg` is required and
   a tampered body is refused.
5. **`eager` is pinned on BOTH legs**, with the resolved kernel printed every
   run. A default that is never printed is a decision nobody made.
