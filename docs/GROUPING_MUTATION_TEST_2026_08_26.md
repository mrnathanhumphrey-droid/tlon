- sensory `hrix` — the ear is the source; direct acquisition

**Every cell involving `hrix` flips on this**, and the frozen lexicon's gloss does

## Claim B — `mar` (remembered) is Marginal, not evidential-proper

**FORCED.** Not because Aikhenvald's five omit memory, but for a reason internal
to what memory is: **remembering is not a source, it is a mode of retention.** You
remember what you originally *saw*, *heard*, or *were told* — so `mar` never
specifies an acquisition, it specifies *later access to one*. Inversion attempt:
"some languages grammaticalize from-memory vs currently-perceiving as a
source-type." True, and it still doesn't make memory a source — it makes it a
tense-like operator over one. The inverted story is materially weaker. **Forced.**

⭐ **A HYPOTHESIS, OFFERED AS A TARGET AND NOT AS A RESULT:** if memory is
parasitic on a prior evidential, then `mar` is a **second-order operator over
another M-value**, not a first-order sibling of them — a different *type*, not a
different *category*. That would mean `mar` does not sit in the relation
alongside the others at all; it **composes** with them. Marked as unshipped: it
is my derivation, not the typology's, and it predicts things the periphery
derivation can test.

## Claim C — `hrin`/`nem` are Judgement, split transitional/terminal

**C1 — judgement, not evidentiality: FORCED.** Denying and doubting express
*commitment to the proposition*, not *source of information*. Chung & Timberlake
and Bybee place judgement modality as a **sibling** of evidentiality under
propositional modality.

⛔⛔ **C2 — the transitional/terminal split: REFUTED AS STATED.** You flagged this
as the one most likely to be your pattern-match. It is, and here is the specific
break:

McGrath's distinction is between **suspension of judgment** (transitional) and
**agnosticism** (terminal). **Both are forms of NEUTRALITY** — believing neither
`p` nor `¬p`. **`nem` (denied) is not neutrality.** Denial is *positive commitment
to `¬p`* — it is **disbelief**, which McGrath explicitly lists as a third thing:
*"like belief and disbelief and unlike suspension…"*

⇒ **`nem` is not on McGrath's map at all**, so McGrath cannot license the split.
In doxastic terms `nem` is `B(¬p)` — structurally a *belief*, with a negated
argument. **It is not a withdrawn state; it is a full commitment pointed the
other way.**

⭐ **THE SPLIT SURVIVES; ITS REASON DOES NOT — AND THE CORRECTED REASON MAKES IT
A SHARPER SPLIT.** `hrin`/`nem` do not divide into *transitional vs terminal*.
They divide into **a neutrality (`hrin`) and a commitment (`nem`)**, which is
forced by what denial means and needs no borrowed epistemology.

⛔ **THE GENERALIZABLE SHAPE, because this is the second instance today:** a
two-member block was mapped onto a two-pole distinction *because both had two
members*. **Two-and-two is not a mapping.** (Object 3 was the same move: two
things called "certainty".)

## Claim E — `xos` (dreamt) is Off-map

**DEFENSIBLE-EITHER-WAY. Marked, not shipped.** Off-map is right for Earth
typology — Aikhenvald's five have no dream category. But the label imports a

source has the classical Tlönian philosophy holding the world to be mental, and

happening is constituted in the holding of it, **a dream is not obviously a
lesser source than an eye** — and if it is not, `xos` is evidential-proper and
the core is five forms, not four.

⇒ This one cannot be settled by typology, because the typology is Earth's. **It
is a ruling about Tlön's metaphysics and it is yours.** Flagged as the highest-
leverage open question in the grouping: it changes the size of the core.

> `m → m′` is smooth iff **`m′` can take `m` as its ground** — if a happening

Directed by construction, no d(·,·), and it *discriminates* rather than blankets.
