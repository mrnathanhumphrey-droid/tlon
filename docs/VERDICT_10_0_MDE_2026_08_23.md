## 10.2 — branch 1 is an EQUIVALENCE claim, and that is the whole problem

⭐⭐ **"Re-climbs to the same level" is not a difference claim.** Branches 2 and 4
(*different level*, *no re-climb*) need the CI to **exclude zero** — the MDE
applies directly and they are detectable above 4.40 pts. Branch 1 asserts
`post_final = pre`, which requires **excluding a difference you would care
about**, i.e. the CI half-width must sit *below* that margin. Strictly harder.

| claim | needs |
|---|---|
| "same level" to ±25 % (2.12 pts) | **n ≈ 14** |
| "same level" to ±50 % (4.23 pts) | n ≈ 6 |
| at n = 5 | half-width is **52 %** of the gap |

> **BRANCH 1 IS UNDERPOWERED-BY-CONSTRUCTION AT n=5.** "Same level to within
> ±half the gap" is not a conservation claim in any useful sense.

⭐ **But the test is still worth running, because it is asymmetric: it can KILL
conservation without being able to earn it.** Branches 2 and 4 are detectable,
and on the evidence so far they are also the likelier outcomes. The cheap result
is the one that is reachable.
