## ⛔⛔ THE CORRECTION THAT MATTERS MOST

**The founding symptom never happened.** `logging_nan_inf_filter` defaults to
`True`: when the loss is NaN or Inf, transformers **substitutes the running
average and logs that**. So the first run's "loss 7.548 → 0" was the filter, not
a collapse — the loss went NaN and the substituted average decayed toward zero.

Hours of hypotheses (degenerate objective, label path) were explaining a logging
artifact. **And the first version of the step trace inherited the same flaw** —
it read the loss from `on_log`, so its row 13 showed `loss=0.6404, finite=True`
while every gradient in the model was already gone. *The trace was showing the
mask.*

⭐ Fixed structurally: the raw loss is captured from the model output by
wrapping `compute_loss`, `logging_nan_inf_filter` is off while tracing, and the
framework's value is retained under the key **`loss_LOGGED_FILTERED`**. The
field name `loss` no longer exists in a trace row.

⭐⭐ **The rule to carry: a framework's logged or summarised value is a proxy;
the value the model returned is the arbiter, and the two diverge exactly when
something is wrong — which is precisely when anyone is looking.**

| step | forward | backward | after optimizer |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ 12 | clean; activation absmax 8128 (`L3.mlp.down_proj`), stable | finite; all 168 gradients small and DECLINING (max 0.19) | clean |

⛔ **The 1-D/2-D fingerprint was never a localiser.** The "70 weights and not
168" split describes which optimizer-state path tolerates a NaN gradient —
tensors below `min_8bit_size` (4096) take the poisoned update on the 32-bit path,
the 98 matrices ride bitsandbytes' 8-bit blockwise path and absorb it. Four
hypotheses were built on that split before the trace showed all 168 gradients
were already gone.

⛔ **And "all 168 at once" was not one event globalised, either** — 159 of them
were independently NaN inside the backward. Both readings mistook a downstream
artifact for a cause; the arrival-order cut is the first evidence that points at
an origin rather than at an aftermath.

## ⛔ SEVEN MECHANISMS EXCLUDED BY POSITIVE TEST

1. **8-bit quantisation of the 1-D params** — they never used 8-bit state;
   `min_8bit_size` is 4096 and every 1-D tensor here is 3584 or 512.
2. **Size-dependent NaN propagation** — one NaN gradient destroys 512, 3584 and
   8192 alike.
3. **Second-moment underflow** — 300 steps at gradient 1e-12, 1e-20 and exactly
   0 all stay finite.
4. **The label path** — 1297/2216 positions unmasked; `pad_token` (151643) ≠
   `eos` (151645). Corpus scan: **0 zero-token, 0 truncated**, lengths 55–317
   against a 384 cap — re-verified locally 2026-09-06 against a byte-identical
   rebuild (sha `dd40e22f85b0b6e4`).
   ⚠️ **The row count in this line was wrong: it read 20,002.** The corpus is
   **60,148 train / 1,227 eval**, counted off the verified rebuild. The scan's
   FINDINGS reproduce exactly; only its denominator was wrong, and the
   provenance of 20,002 could not be recovered. See [[resolution_match]].
5. **The assembly at toy scale** — 16-cell ablation over mixed-dtype ×
   checkpointing × input-require-grads × autocast: no NaN anywhere, loss
   descends identically in all 12 runnable cells.
6. **Checkpointing × frozen stack, at real scale** — with gradient checkpointing
   OFF *and* `enable_input_require_grads()` not called, the failure is
   byte-identical: gradient non-finite at **step 13**, **168 tensors**, same
   first-tensor list.

7. **The fully-masked attention row** — `padding_side` is right, and the
   minimum attendable keys over every (batch, query) pair is **1, never 0**,
   measured locally against a byte-identical corpus rebuild. Costed $0.

⭐ **The determinism is the asset.** Two runs, different assemblies, identical to
the step. This is not a flaky failure being sampled — it is a fixed event, so the
instrument can be pointed rather than swept.

⛔ **"bf16 overflow" is now EXCLUDED, not merely discouraged.** bf16 has the
*same exponent range as fp32*, and the post-clip signature settles it: an `inf`
origin would have left every other tensor at exactly zero and finite, which is
not what the run reported. See §6 of `FINDINGS_FULLFT_TRACE_2026_09_06.md`.

⛔ **A third instrument bug, fixed while building the probe.**
`ForwardProbe.snapshot()` answered outside its own window, so **rows 15–19 of the
raw-loss trace are five identical copies of step 14** wearing later step numbers.
They are not measurements. ⭐ Three for three: *an instrument that keeps
answering after it stops looking reports its own memory as data.*
